### Bright Coffee Shop - Sales Performance Analysis

## Executive Summary
This Power BI dashboard analyzes sales revenue across three store locations (Hell's Kitchen, Astoria, and Lower Manhattan) from January to June. Total revenue generated across all branches reached **$698.81K**.

![Bright Coffee Shop Dashboard](path-to-your-dashboard-image.png)

## Key Insights
* **Revenue Trend**: Monthly revenue experienced steady growth from January through June.
* **Top Product Category**: **Coffee** drives the vast majority of sales (38.63%), followed by **Tea** (28.11%) and **Bakery** items (11.70%).
* **Daily Performance**: Sales revenue remains consistently balanced throughout the week, peaking slightly on Mondays ($101.68K) and Fridays ($101.37K).
* **Peak Hours**: Revenue peaks heavily during **Rush Hour** and **Mid-Morning** operational shifts across all locations.
* **Customer Spending**: The **Medium** spend bucket accounts for the highest proportion of transactions.

## Data Pipeline & Modeling Highlights
* Integrated transactional sales datasets across 3 store locations.
* Created custom DAX calculated columns (`MonthOrder`, `DayOrder`) to handle explicit chronological sorting logic for time-based charts.
* Standardized time classification dimensions (Rush Hour, Mid-Morning, Afternoon, Night) for peak hour capacity analysis.
